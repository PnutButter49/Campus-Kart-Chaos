How to play

Drive

W/↑ accelerate
S/↓ brake & reverse
A/← steer left
D/→ steer right

Drift

Hold SPACE while turning. Build sparks, release for a boost. Longer clean drifts give stronger boosts.

Items

Drive through glowing item boxes. Press E to use your item. Comeback items are stronger when you're behind.

Pause

P pauses offline races. Online races continue live, so P only opens the race menu.
